Privacy Policy

EventEdge · Effective October 27, 2026

EventEdge ("we", "us") is event-production management software published by Kevin Shea. This policy explains what information EventEdge collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the EventEdge web application and its QuickBooks Online integration.

Information we collect

  • Account information — name, email address and sign-in credentials for users of your organization's EventEdge account.
  • Business data you enter — projects, events, contacts, crew, equipment, quotes, orders and related records.
  • QuickBooks Online data — when an administrator connects QuickBooks Online, EventEdge reads and writes only the accounting records needed to keep the two systems in step: customers, vendors, items, accounts, invoices, payments, purchase orders and bills, plus your company's QuickBooks identifier.
  • Technical data — server logs (IP address, browser type, timestamps, errors) used to operate and secure the service.

How we use information

  • To provide the service: run your organization's account and sync the records you choose with QuickBooks Online.
  • To secure, troubleshoot and improve the service.
  • To contact you about your account or the service.

We do not sell personal information, do not use your data for advertising, and do not share QuickBooks data with any third party except as needed to run the service (for example, our hosting provider) or as required by law.

QuickBooks Online connection

EventEdge connects to QuickBooks Online through Intuit's OAuth 2.0 authorization. We never see or store your Intuit password. The access and refresh tokens Intuit issues are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) and used only to perform the sync your organization has set up. You can disconnect at any time from EventEdge's accounting settings or from your Intuit account; disconnecting revokes the tokens and stops all syncing.

Data storage and security

Data is stored with a commercial cloud hosting provider in the United States. Each organization's data is isolated from every other organization's at the database level. Connections use HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the data.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your organization's account is active. When an account is closed, or on request from an account owner, we delete the organization's data, including stored QuickBooks tokens and synced references, within 30 days, except where we must keep records to meet legal obligations. Records already written into your QuickBooks company remain in QuickBooks under your control.

Your choices

Account owners can view, correct, export or delete their organization's data, and can disconnect QuickBooks Online at any time. To make a request, email us at the address below.

Children

EventEdge is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here with a new effective date, and notify account owners of material changes.
